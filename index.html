#InAmigos · Who We Are

An NGO Built on Compassion & Collective Action

Founded by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation believes that every individual deserves dignity, opportunity, and a clean planet. In a world that often feels divided, we stand as a bridge — uniting volunteers, corporates, and communities around shared purpose.

We address the root causes of inequality: lack of education, hunger, deforestation, gender disparity, and neglect of animals. With a programmatic and systematic approach, our teams operate across Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and beyond.

ISO 9001:2015 80G & 12A Certified CSR-1 Registered NITI Aayog NGO Darpan Section 8 | Govt. Licensed

#IAFImpact · Why It Matters

The Change We Are Creating

Every rupee, every hour of volunteering, every sapling planted — ripples outward into communities, families, and futures.

Bridging the Education Gap

Millions of children in India cannot access quality education due to poverty. Bachpanshala brings learning to their doorstep, free of cost.

Fighting Hunger Daily

Project SEVA conducts regular food distribution drives, ensuring hot meals and warm clothing reach the most vulnerable on our streets.

Uplifting Women

UDAAN trains women in marketable skills, helping them achieve financial independence and break cycles of poverty — one skill at a time.

Healing the Planet

PRAKRITI volunteers plant trees, run clean-up drives, and educate communities about sustainability to protect India's natural heritage.

Giving Animals a Voice

JEEV rescues injured strays, organises feeding drives, and advocates for animal protection laws — because every life has value.

Building Tomorrow's Workforce

VIKAS has trained over 30,000 interns in digital and professional skills, unlocking livelihoods and careers across 28 Indian states.
